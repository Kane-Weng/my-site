<h2 class="section-header build">Mission</h2>

This research develops a "Visual Physics Dataset" by taking existing 3D models from online datasets and annotating them with physical parameters, such as mass, density, and friction, to make them fully usable in physics-based simulators like Isaac Sim. This work supports broader research into physically grounded multi-modal AI pretraining.
